Our Four Core Pillars

Family

Family is the foundation of a child's emotional and social development. This pillar focuses on stories and content that celebrate love, connection, respect, and healthy relationships within families. Through relatable characters and everyday experiences, children learn the importance of kindness, communication, and supporting one another.

Self-Reliance

This pillar encourages children to develop independence, confidence, and problem-solving skills. Through empowering stories and lessons, children are guided to trust their abilities, take initiative, and understand that they are capable of overcoming challenges and making positive choices.

Education

Education is presented as an exciting journey of discovery and growth. This pillar promotes curiosity, learning, creativity, and critical thinking. The content supports children in developing important academic and life skills while nurturing a love for learning that extends beyond the classroom.

Positive self image

Positive self-image focuses on helping children appreciate and value who they are. Through uplifting stories and affirming messages, this pillar teaches children to embrace their uniqueness, build self-confidence, and develop a strong sense of self-worth.

Our story

We're here for the little ones with big dreams - the kids who dance in the aisles at the supermarket, ask a thousand questions before 9am, and light up every room with their magic.

We are a South African publisher creating books and stories that sound like our homes, look like our children, and celebrate the full, beautiful spectrum of who they are. Because our kids deserve to see themselves as heroes, explorers, kings, queens - and just as they are: enough.

We started this because we were tired of searching for stories where our children truly belonged. Tired of "diversity" being an afterthought. So we built something bold. Something ours. Something that says:

"You matter. You are powerful. You are made for greatness."

Our mission? To remind our children of colour - every shade, every curl, every accent - that their stories are worth telling. That joy, pride, and softness live here too. That they are not just part of the future - they are the future.

This is more than publishing. It's purpose.
It's love on every page.
It's representation with roots.

We are loud about it. Proud about it.
We are for little people. Always have been. Always will be.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Vision

At For Little People, our vision is to create a world where every child feels valued, supported, and inspired to explore their potential.

We believe that the earliest years of life are the foundation for a lifetime of learning, confidence, and creativity. Our goal is to nurture curious minds and compassionate hearts by providing an environment where children feel safe, encouraged, and excited to discover the world around them.

Through meaningful experiences, playful exploration, and thoughtful guidance, we aim to help every child grow into a confident, capable individual who is ready to embrace the future.

Our Mission

Our mission is to support the healthy development of young children by creating experiences, resources, and environments that encourage learning, creativity, and emotional growth.

We are committed to:

  • Nurturing curiosity by encouraging children to explore, ask questions, and learn through play.
  • Supporting holistic development, including social, emotional, cognitive, and physical growth.
  • Creating safe and joyful spaces where children feel respected, included, and valued.
  • Partnering with parents and families to support every child's journey of discovery and development.
  • Building strong foundations for the future, helping children develop confidence, resilience, and a love for learning.

What Drives Us

Everything we do is guided by a simple belief: every child deserves the opportunity to thrive.

By fostering creativity, kindness, and independence from an early age, we help lay the groundwork for children to become thoughtful individuals and engaged members of their communities.

At For Little People, we celebrate the magic of childhood and the limitless possibilities that come with it.

I am Beautiful and So Are You!

Meet Layla - a bright, bold little girl who knows exactly who she is.

She's joyful. She's curious. She's confident.
And most of all, she's deeply in love with the skin she's in.

Layla doesn't just celebrate her own beauty - she honours the beauty she sees in every child around her.

From deep brown to honey gold, coily crowns to soft twists, wide noses to gap-toothed grins - she sees it all, and she sees it as good.

Because to Layla, different doesn't mean better or worse - it simply means unique.

The Story of a Hundred Dresses

Story of a hundred dresses: This vibrant, interactive book follows Layla as she plays with her animal friends.

Designed to engage young readers, it blends early learning with cultural discovery by using the unique patterns and colours of five traditional tribes to teach teaching counting and visual recognition. As children turn the pages, they don't just learn their 123s; they explore the rich heritage of the South African people through beautiful, cultural illustrations.

The Story of Bumble the Bee

This is the tale of Bumble, a little bee who buzzes from friend to friend, searching for fun and laughter.

But no matter how hard he tries, things do not quite go as planned. Through his adventures, Bumble learns that being alone does not mean being lonely. Sometimes, the sweetest kind of happiness comes from enjoying your own company, and when you do, others are drawn to your joyful buzz!

Bumble's story is a gentle reminder for little readers that joy, confidence, and friendship often begin with learning to enjoy your own company.

"A buzzing tale about finding joy within!"

The Perfect Christmas

A Heartwarming Story of Friendship, Family and Love

Christmas is just around the corner, and the excitement is in the air! Join Layla, Mulalo and Jabu as they meet at the park for a fun-filled playdate, sharing their favourite Christmas memories, family traditions and special hopes for the festive season.

As the young friends talk about how their families celebrate Christmas, they discover that no two celebrations are exactly alike. From delicious family feasts and exchanging gifts to singing Christmas songs and enjoying precious moments together, each family has its own beautiful way of making Christmas special.

Rooted in the warmth and joy of South African family life, this heartwarming story celebrates friendship, diversity and the unique traditions that bring families together. It gently reminds young readers that there is no single way to have the perfect Christmas, because what matters most is being surrounded by the people who love and care for us.

A delightful festive story that encourages children to appreciate their own family traditions while embracing the beautiful differences that make every Christmas special.

Hosi and the Whales

An Inspiring Ocean Adventure of Discovery, Bravery and Marine Wonder

Dive into an enchanting ocean journey with Hosi, an adventurous young boy who dreams of the great blue sea! When Hosi sets off on a marvelous marine voyage, he meets gentle giant whales dancing through the waves beneath the sunny South African skies.

Together with his majestic ocean friends, Hosi discovers the wondrous beauty of sea creatures, the importance of protecting our oceans, and the bravery found inside every child when exploring the wonders of nature.

Brimming with vibrant coastal wonder and joy, this breathtaking tale inspires young hearts to dream big, embrace adventure, and cherish the precious magic of our oceans and marine life.

A breathtaking ocean adventure that encourages children to explore fearlessly, care for our oceans, and discover the wonder within our world.
